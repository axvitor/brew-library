Overview

Brew Library design system

The tokens, components and patterns of the Brew Library app as it ships today, in the F4 “Evening” direction. Every value here was taken from the app's own stylesheet, and every class keeps the app's name, so this page and the code describe the same thing. Use it to build new screens that look and behave like the rest of the app.

Principles

These are the decisions the app already makes consistently. New work should keep them.

One accent, rationed

Burnt orange (--accent-timer #C84A16) is kept for the brew action, adding a recipe and the primary button. Everything else is espresso greys.

Thin, oversized numbers

Measurements (grams, times, ratios) are set light or thin and large with tabular figures. They are never bold.

Pills and circles

Buttons, tabs, chips and search are fully rounded. Cards use large radii (20–28 px). Nothing is square except printed paper.

Controls float as glass

Anything that stays put while the page scrolls (the nav capsule, the brew bar, form actions, Close) is frosted glass over the content.

Sentence-case labels

Labels are small, muted and in sentence case. There are no tracked uppercase eyebrows. Hierarchy comes from size and weight.

Drawn, not decorated

Illustration is line drawing in the same stroke as the icons: brewers, the bean, the cup. There are no photos, emoji or gradient washes.

Colors

Semantic tokens are what components use. The ramps underneath are the raw values. Each swatch shows the live value for the current theme, with HEX and RGB for both themes.

Accent — roast

The single brand accent. 600 carries white text at about 4.9:1. 300 is the accent text and line colour on dark grounds; in the light theme it darkens to #B23F0F to keep 4.5:1 on white.

Neutrals

Espresso greys for grounds, surfaces, borders and text. The app still names this ramp --pine-* for historical reasons; the values are the ones shown.

Typography

One family, Albert Sans, for everything. Display sizes are regular or light and tracked in. Numbers are light or thin and always tabular. The app loads weights 200–800.

Do
  • Use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums for every number that changes or lines up.
  • Keep labels in sentence case, 13 px medium, muted.
  • Let large display text go light (300) and tracked (−0.02 to −0.035em).
Don't
  • Bold a measurement. Thin or light weights carry the numbers.
  • Add tracked uppercase eyebrows. The only uppercase is the stamp ring and the bag label roaster.
  • Introduce a second typeface.

Borders are 1 px hairlines; 1.5 px is kept for drawn marks (step dots, the bean, the bag valve). Radii grow with the size of the thing.

Shadows & glass

On the dark ground depth comes mostly from surface steps (app → card → sunken). Shadows are kept for things that float. Glass is for controls that stay fixed over scrolling content.

Glass recipe

Four layers: the backdrop blurred and saturated; a thin tint so icons keep contrast; a specular rim (bright top-left and bottom-right); a soft sheen across the top. Users who ask for less transparency get a solid surface.

.glass
.glass { background: radial-gradient(120% 70% at 30% -20%, rgba(255,255,255,.14), transparent 60%), var(--glass-tint); backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(190%) brightness(1.08); box-shadow: var(--glass-shadow); /* + ::before specular rim */ }

Grids

Lists are single column on a phone. The library list becomes an auto-fill grid on wide screens. Recipe tiles split the row equally. Form rows use 2-up grids with a 14px gap.

Equal tracks: grid-template-columns: repeat(N, minmax(0, 1fr)), gap 8px (tiles) or 14px (form rows).

Motion

Short, settling motion. Controls press down (scale 0.972), cards press less (0.988). The timer's progress is linear and never eases, so it can't misreport elapsed time. Reduced-motion settings switch all of it off.

Accessibility

Focus

Every control shows --shadow-focus, a 2px gap and a 2px accent ring, on keyboard focus only (:focus-visible).

Targets

44×44px minimum. Fields are 48px tall; the nav's items are 52px.

Names

Icon-only buttons carry aria-label. Decorative SVG is aria-hidden. Visually hidden nav labels stay in the markup.

Motion and transparency

prefers-reduced-motion stops animations; prefers-reduced-transparency makes glass solid.

Keyboard patterns

Tabs and segmented controls: arrows move, Home/End jump. Menus and dropdowns: arrows, Enter, Escape. Dialogs trap focus and return it on close.

Contrast

Text pairs are listed under Colors with their ratios. --text-faint is for placeholders and large text only.
